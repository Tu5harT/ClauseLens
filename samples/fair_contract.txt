CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Acme Ltd. ("Client") and Jane Doe ("Consultant").

1. Services
The Consultant shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care in accordance with good industry practice.
Each party shall appoint a representative to act as the primary contact for the project.

2. Fees
Invoices will be issued monthly and are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.
Any change to the fees must be agreed in writing and signed by both parties.

3. Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.
Upon termination the Client shall pay for Services performed up to the termination date.

4. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own, for a period of three years after disclosure.

5. Liability
Neither party's total liability under this Agreement shall exceed the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim.

6. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
